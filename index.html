One partner, the whole journey.

Come in at any stage.

1Assess

Drop in for an audit of your current translations. Send us your loc, AI or human, any language. You get a plain roadmap and a quote to a painless release.

2Prepare

Get your script translation-ready with the right format and context. Messy export, odd format? We sort it, glossary and all, and set you up for next time.

3Translate

Human, in every language your players read, calibrated to your script. Real translators, never machine post-editing. Eleven languages and counting, Chinese and Japanese to the world.

4Voice

Voice your game professionally in Japanese or Chinese, on request. Native voice talent matched to each character's register, recorded, directed, and delivered to your spec.

5Ship

In-game testing up to release, by native-speaker gamers who play it all. Overflow, clipping, wrong variables, leftover source text. After release we watch reviews and flag what needs a fix.
